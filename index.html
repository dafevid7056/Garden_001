Sun setting at Gustavo Uribe Botero Park. It was a saturday and I needed to get some footage for a school project and I've always loved the tall trees from this park. Billing's Arcade in Fort Tyron Park. I was with Helena that day visiting the MET Cloisters. Super tired after walking around the museum and then the park. I was thinking about how crazy it is that some people decided to transport rocks across the ocean to re-build a medieval monastery in the middle of upper Manhattan. Very picturesque place. I particularly liked the courtyards and herbariums. They gave off a lovely smell and you could see butterflies and bumblebees flying around, which made it seem more alive and serene.

The Bearghain is a game we developed with an amazing group of artists (:

The game is set in the fictitious city of Bearlin, where most of the population are bears. Here you play as a kitty, who's disguised as a bear, trying rave for as long as possible at the Bearghain, the famous and most exclusive nightclub in the city. The game is set in a surreal version of the club, with floating platforms and strange creatures. To avoid getting kicked out by the bouncers you must follow hidden rules of behavior to pass off as a local. The highest score a player achieved was a little over 4 minutes.
